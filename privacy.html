Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version. Close shows you people who are physically near you right now. To do that we use your live location while the app is open — but nobody else ever gets your coordinates. Other people only see a rounded distance (to about 15 ft) and a rough direction. We don't sell your data and there are no ads.

Who we are

Close is an independent project run from New York. Questions or requests: shekhardash.study@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We don't get your Google password.
  • Profile: the first name you choose, your illustrated avatar settings, your optional "spot cue" (e.g. "yellow scarf, by the coffee cart"), and research cards you add.
  • Location: your device's latitude and longitude, sent about every 5 seconds while Close is open and visible. We keep only your latest position — each update replaces the last one. Positions older than 2 minutes are hidden from everyone.
  • Activity: messages, group chats, waves, Close requests, answers you give in Here Lab moments, study-together invites and "find each other" signals.
  • Safety: people you block and reports you make.
  • Device settings like your notification choice are stored only on your device.

How location sharing works

  • Your exact coordinates are readable only by you and by our database functions. Other users receive distance rounded to 5 m (about 15 ft) and direction rounded to 15°.
  • You see people within about 900 ft (275 m) and can only message or connect with people within about 500 ft (150 m), unless you're already "Close" with them.
  • Ghost mode immediately deletes your location and stops sharing. Logging out also deletes it.
  • People you've mutually added as Close can see your approximate position on the map when you're both using the app.

How we use it

Only to run Close: showing who's around, delivering messages, running moments and groups, keeping people safe (blocks, reports, abuse prevention) and fixing problems. We don't sell or rent personal information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't share it with data brokers.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and real-time messaging (hosted in the cloud).
  • Google — "Sign in with Google" and web fonts.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the app files.
  • OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap — map tiles. Your browser requests the map area you're looking at.

Credits: 3D emoji are Fluent Emoji © Microsoft, used under the MIT licence (packaged by LobeHub). They're built into the app, so showing them sends nothing to anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Location: only your latest point, deleted on ghost mode or log-out; hidden after 2 minutes without an update.
  • Moments expire after 3 hours; their anonymous answers may be kept for aggregate results.
  • Messages and profile data: until you delete your account.
  • Reports: kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, turn on ghost mode, change who can ping you, block anyone, and log out at any time. To get a copy of your data or delete your account and everything linked to it, email us from the address you sign in with. We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California or other US states with privacy laws), you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your information — we honor those requests for everyone.

Adults only

Close is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and database rules stop users from reading each other's private data. No system is perfect, so please don't put sensitive information in your profile or messages.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
